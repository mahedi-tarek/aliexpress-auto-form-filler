How to install

1
Unzip the downloaded file
Find the downloaded zip in your Downloads folder and extract it. You'll get a folder called AliExpress-Address-Filler.
2
Open Chrome Extensions
In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
3
Enable Developer Mode
Toggle on Developer mode in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.
4
Load the extension
Click Load unpacked, then select the AliExpress-Address-Filler folder you extracted. The extension will appear in your list.
5
Pin it to your toolbar (optional)
Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome's toolbar → find "AliExpress Address Filler" → click the pin icon so it's always visible.

Create your account

Click the extension icon in your toolbar to open the popup, then follow these steps:

✉️
Sign up with your email
Enter the same email you used for payment, and choose a password (minimum 8 characters).
🔢
Verify your email
We'll send a 6-digit code to your inbox. Enter it in the extension popup to confirm your account.
✅
You're in — subscription active
Your subscription is linked to your account. The popup will show "Active" status.

How to use it

1
Copy any address
Select and copy an address from an email, eBay order, spreadsheet — anywhere.
2
Open AliExpress checkout
Go to the delivery address form on AliExpress. A red button appears in the corner of the page.
3
Click "📋 Fill address"
All fields — name, street, city, postcode, country, phone code — fill instantly. Allow clipboard access if Chrome asks (one-time prompt).
