# SEO & keyword research: Address Filler for AliExpress

Researched Nov 24, 2026. Search volumes for this niche are small and no tool publishes reliable Chrome Web Store (CWS) numbers, so keywords are ranked by **intent** and by what competing extensions target. The sources are listed at the bottom.

## Who searches for this
1. **Solo dropshippers** who place AliExpress orders by hand (Shopify, eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop, WooCommerce).
2. **Dropshipping VAs**, who fill dozens of supplier orders a day.
3. **Agencies and fulfillment teams** that run many stores with several VAs. They are the highest-value buyers.
4. **Personal shoppers and resellers** who send gifts or orders to other people's addresses.

## Competitors (what users find today)
| Extension | Angle | Weakness to attack |
|---|---|---|
| AutoDS Helper (about 30k users) | Copies addresses from AutoDS orders | Needs an AutoDS subscription |
| Dropship N Paste | General address autofill on any site | Not AliExpress-specific |
| Shopify to AliExpress Fast Orders | Shopify only | Last updated in 2020, 2 ratings |
| eBay to AliExpress helpers | eBay only | Low ratings (1★ on 51 ratings) |
| DSers / SellersDash | Full automation platforms | Heavy, store-connected, pricier |

**Our position:** works from *any* copied text, needs no store connection, keeps data private, and has team seats for VA agencies.

## Rules (from Google's CWS guidance)
- No keyword stuffing or repeated keyword lists. Google can suspend listings for it.
- After launch, the main ranking levers are **title keywords**, **installs and weekly users**, and **ratings**. Ask happy users for a review after their 10th fill (a possible future feature).
