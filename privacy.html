Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Address Filler for AliExpress ("the extension", "we") is a Chrome extension that fills the delivery-address form on AliExpress from an address you copied. This policy explains what data the extension handles and why. Address Filler for AliExpress is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by AliExpress or Alibaba Group.

Data we collect

  • Account information: your email address, an optional username, and your password, which our authentication provider (Supabase) stores in hashed form. We need these so you can sign in and so we can manage your free trial and subscription.
  • Subscription status: whether you are on a trial or subscribed, your trial end date, and the Stripe customer and subscription IDs linked to your account.
  • Device identifier: a random ID created when you install the extension, plus a short label such as "Chrome on macOS". We use it to count the computers (seats) your plan allows and to allow one free trial per computer.
  • Network fingerprint for trial abuse prevention: when a new free trial starts, we store a one-way keyed hash of your IP address (never the IP itself). It is used only to limit how many free trials can be started from one network, and is deleted with your account.
  • Plan details: which plan you are on (Solo, Team, Agency or Agency Plus) and how many computers it covers.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Addresses and clipboard contents. The extension reads your clipboard only when you click the "Paste & Fill" button. The address is parsed and filled into the page entirely inside your browser. It is never sent to our servers or to anyone else.
  • Your browsing history, the pages you visit, or your AliExpress account and order data.
  • Payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Website cookies and advertising

Our website (autoformfiller.xyz, not the extension) uses the Meta Pixel to measure how our ads perform, for example how many visitors click "Add to Chrome" or complete a purchase. It only runs after you click Accept on the cookie banner; if you decline, no tracking cookies are set. Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. The extension itself contains no advertising trackers.

How we use data

We use the data above only to sign you in, check whether your trial or subscription is active, enforce device limits, and send service emails (for example, a receipt or a failed-payment notice). We do not sell or rent your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Service providers

  • Supabase: account authentication and database.
  • Cloudflare: hosts our licensing API.
  • Stripe: payment processing and subscription management.
  • Resend: transactional email delivery.
  • Meta Platforms: ad measurement on our website only, with your consent.

These providers process data only on our behalf to run the service.

Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. On your computer the extension keeps your sign-in session, your button-position preference and your device ID in Chrome's extension storage. Signing out removes the session from your browser.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account exists. To delete your account and all related data, email us from your account email address and we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Significant changes will also be announced inside the extension.
